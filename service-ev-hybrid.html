Safe 12V Auxiliary & Low-Voltage Power for Hybrid & EVs

A surprising fact for many electric and hybrid vehicle owners: even with a fully charged 400V or 800V lithium traction battery, a dead 12-volt auxiliary battery will completely disable your Tesla, Prius, Taycan, or Ioniq. The car cannot close its high-voltage contactors without 12V power.

VoltFix Pro technicians are high-voltage safety certified (NFPA 70E & IMI Level 3/4). We service 12V AGM/LiFePO4 auxiliary batteries, DC-to-DC converters, BMS communication lines, and thermal coolant pumps without risking your vehicle's high-voltage warranty.

Our 4-Stage Diagnostic Workflow

STAGE 01

Conductance & CCA Test

Sends a low-frequency AC signal through the battery cells to measure active lead surface area and actual Cold Cranking Amps available.

STAGE 02

Cranking Voltage Drop

Measures the instantaneous voltage dip when the starter engages. If voltage drops below 9.6V at room temperature, the plates cannot hold peak amperage.

STAGE 03

Alternator Diode Ripple

Verifies that the charging system produces clean Direct Current (DC). A blown alternator diode leaks AC voltage, causing battery boil-over.

STAGE 04

ECU Battery Coding (IBS)

Programs the new battery Ah rating into BMW, Mercedes, Audi, and Ford computer modules so the charging curve resets to zero.
