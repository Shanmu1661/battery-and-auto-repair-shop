Established 2011

15 Years of Automotive Electrical Innovation

VoltFix Pro started with a simple belief: automobile electrical systems should never be a mystery of trial-and-error part swapping. Founded by two aerospace electrical technicians who applied aviation-grade wire harness standards to vehicles, we have grown into the region's leading auto electrical diagnostic workshop and 24/7 doorstep dispatch service.

Today, our certified team handles over 1,200 battery installations and 400 deep electrical diagnostics every single month across everyday passenger cars, European performance vehicles, commercial delivery fleets, and emerging hybrid/EV platforms.

The VoltFix Standard

How We Outperform Ordinary Repair Shops

Four non-negotiable engineering principles applied to every passenger car, fleet van, and electric vehicle.

Standard 01

Aviation-Grade Splices

Zero twist caps or scotchlocks. We exclusively use heat-shrink adhesive solder sleeves and weather-pack sealed connectors rated for 150°C engine bays.

Standard 02

Oscilloscope Diagnostics

We measure microsecond voltage drops and CAN-bus waveform packets directly. No replacing parts on guesswork at the customer's expense.

Standard 03

100% Closed-Loop Recycling

Over 99.4% of every lead-acid, AGM, and spent lithium battery core we remove is safely recycled through EPA-certified circular smelters.

Standard 04

Digital Proof Reports

Every customer receives a complete digital health report with before-and-after oscilloscope captures, CCA readings, and thermal photos.
