Doorstep Battery & Roadside FAQs

Everything you need to know about our mobile arrival, battery registration, and warranty claims.

Our fleet of 14 mobile service vans are continuously deployed across key metro quadrants. Our average arrival time across the metropolitan area is under 25 minutes from the moment you confirm your dispatch.
Modern European, American, and Japanese vehicles equipped with Start-Stop (such as BMW, Audi, Mercedes, Ford, and Mazda) track battery age. If a new battery isn't registered into the vehicle's engine control module (ECM), the alternator continues charging it aggressively as if it were old, destroying the new battery in under 12 months. We register every new battery with diagnostic tablets.
We never sell a battery blindly. Before removing your old battery, the technician connects a digital conductance analyzer to test the battery's internal plates, followed by a charging circuit and starter draw test. If the alternator is the culprit, we explain the diagnostic data and can replace or rebuild the alternator right in your driveway.
Yes! Standard tow trucks often cannot fit into low-clearance basement garages (below 6'8"). Our technicians carry portable high-amperage jump units, mobile diagnostic kits, and battery dollies that fit into any residential or commercial underground parking space.
Absolutely. Lead-acid and AGM batteries are 99% recyclable. We safely transport your spent battery to an EPA-licensed recycling facility, saving you the hassle and the core deposit disposal fee.
